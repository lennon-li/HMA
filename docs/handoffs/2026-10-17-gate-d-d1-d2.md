## D1 — contracts (no authority yet)

- `hma.acceptance-contract/v1` (`internal/model/contract.go`): run, intent, non-goals, criteria
  `{id, statement, check{executable, argv, working_dir}, threshold, evidence_requirement}`, risk,
  deliverables, budget ceilings, permitted routes, permission envelope, policy digest. No results.
  Digest = SHA-256 of the canonical re-serialization of what HMA read. Strict decoding: one document,
  unique plain lowercase keys, no unknown fields.
- `ApprovalBinding.acceptance_contract_digest` (additive, omitted when empty; existing digests unchanged).
- Transition check (`engine.EvaluateContractBinding`, called by `transition.Apply`):
  `ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA -> PLANNING` with a digest needs the document; HMA recomputes the digest, requires
  the run, and requires the contract's criterion ids to equal the core criterion records exactly. Every
  later approval must carry the bound digest; a presented contract must hash to it (a changed threshold
  under the same ids is refused). A run that used a contract cannot accept criteria again without one; a
  run without a contract is unchanged and cannot be given one after Planning.
- `hma.plan/v1`: §10 unit fields, per-unit `max_attempts`, `max_wall_minutes`, `cost_ceiling`, other
  budgets, dependencies, `policy_digest`, bound `acceptance_contract_digest`. Its digest is the approvals'
  `accepted_plan_digest`. Validation: unique unit ids, known dependencies, acyclic graph, positive lease
  ceilings; against the contract: same contract, run, and policy, known criteria, unit ceilings within
  the contract's.

## D2 — lease derivation (`engine.DeriveLease`, pure)

Inputs: core chain, presented plan and contract, current policy digest, counters, now. No host record is
an input, so a host-written lease grants nothing. Issuer: the unit's latest approved transition into
`IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION` from `ROUTE_SELECTION` or `INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION`. The plan must hash to
its `accepted_plan_digest` and contain the unit; the contract must be the bound one and the plan within
it. Generation = issuer sequence and anchor. Active only while: run not terminal; no later approved
transition of the unit or the whole run out of `IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION`; no later return of a
transitive plan dependency to an earlier gate; `now < issued + max_wall_minutes` (half-open); counters
below ceilings; policy digest equals the plan's. Lease derivation is not yet wired to a command; D3
(`hma host attempt`) consumes it.

**Not changed here:** host commands that do not present a plan keep the conservative rule (any unit's
return to Planning or earlier voids every route approval). That rule is stricter than the plan graph.

## D1+D2 r1 remediation

Plans require every §10 field (non-empty lists; dependencies present, may be empty), a complexity budget,
per-unit permission classes, and a bound contract digest; unit routes must be contract permitted routes,
unit permissions inside the contract envelope, every contract criterion advanced by some unit, and wall-time
ceilings at most one year. Contract and plan bytes must be valid UTF-8 without lone surrogate escapes. A
contract presented with an approval that names none is refused. Leases: wall time runs from the approval
timestamp and no lease exists before it; a dependency's `INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION -> IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION`
correction voids dependents; the core criterion set must equal the contract's; `lease_digest` covers
{transition digest, unit, plan digest}; negative counters are refused.

## D1+D2 r2 remediation

Every contract and plan field is required and non-null (checked against the raw document before use); an
empty list is written `[]` and a zero count `0`. Check `argv` must be present.

## D1+D2 r3 remediation

A later approved return to `GROUNDING` or `ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA` voids every lease, whatever unit the record
names. Returns to `PLANNING` or `ROUTE_SELECTION` stay scoped by the plan graph. (Refusing unit tags on
whole-run rewinds at the transition boundary is not done here; the lease rule does not depend on it.)

## D1+D2 r4 remediation

Plan route strings are route identities (`host.RouteIdentity`: the digest of the exact route without its
approval reference). `DeriveLease` requires the selected route's projection (`model.RouteGrant`, built by
`host.RouteGrant`): identity equal to the unit's implementer route, permission classes non-empty and inside
the unit's grant, and approval digest equal to the unit's latest `ROUTE_SELECTION -> IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION`
transition digest at or before the issuer (so an Independent validation correction keeps it). An absent
projection voids the lease. D3 builds the projection from the host route preflight.

## D1+D2 r5 remediation

A lease is void (`LEASE_VOID_AFTER_WAIVER_CHANGE`) when a waiver its issuing approval bound (`active_waivers`,
waiver-operation record digests) is withdrawn, expired, re-granted, or changed by a legacy waiver record
after the issuer, using the `EvaluateWaiverChange` packet rule. A bound digest that names no earlier grant
voids it too. A later regrant does not revive the generation.

## D1+D2 r6 remediation

Each bound waiver digest must name the live grant of its scope at the issuer (waiver history replayed up to
the issuer; withdrawal, expiry, replacement, or a legacy waiver record ends a grant) and appear once.

## D1+D2 r7 remediation

For a correction issuer (`INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION -> IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZATION`), the retained route approval
is checked with the same waiver rule through the whole chain; if it is invalid the lease is void, with no
fallback to an older route approval. A fresh Route selection approval is required.

## D1+D2 r8 remediation

For a correction issuer, the rewind and dependency scan starts at the retained route approval, not at the
correction: any return to an earlier gate by the unit or the whole run, any Grounding or Acceptance criteria
return under any unit tag, and any dependency rewind or correction between the route approval and now voids
the lease. Only the unit's own forward walk back to the correction is skipped. The route approval must also
bind the presented plan digest. No fallback to an older route approval.
